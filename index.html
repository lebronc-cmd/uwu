Frequently Asked Questions

 
❓
 

Find answers to common questions about our platform

 
 
What is the LunaLaunch?

LunaLaunch is a powerful platform that allows you to create and deploy Solana tokens effortlessly. Our platform provides all the tools you need to launch your token project successfully.

 
 
How can I create a token on the Solana blockchain?

Creating a token is simple! Just connect your wallet, fill in the token details like name, symbol, and supply, upload an optional logo, and click "Create Token". Our platform handles all the technical complexity for you.

 
 
How can I manage token authorities on Solana?

Token authorities control who can mint new tokens and freeze accounts. You can choose to enable or disable mint authority and freeze authority when creating your token. These settings provide security and control over your token.

 
 
What support resources are available if I encounter issues during my token launch?

We provide comprehensive support including documentation, live chat support, and a ticket system. You can contact our support team anytime through the floating support button or create a support ticket for detailed assistance.

 
 
Do I need any programming skills to launch a token on your platform?

No programming skills required! Our platform is designed to be user-friendly with a visual interface. Simply fill out the form with your token details, and our system handles all the technical blockchain interactions automatically.

 
 
How can I confirm that my token launch was successful?

After launch, you will receive a confirmation with your token details and blockchain transaction hash. You can also view your token in the Solana explorer and see it in your wallet. Our platform provides real-time status updates throughout the process.
